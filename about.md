I'm Ben — a New Zealander living in Saitama, Japan. There are two things that
people know me for: riding bikes, and being a bit of a (computer) geek. I have
a background in finance which has taken me around the world from New Zealand and
Australia to London and Hong Kong, and now Japan.

Nowadays, family is the part of my life that matters most, and a good chunk of it
happens around a table: board games with my kids, several nights a week, with
an Uno deck that is as much sellotape as card.

Python is my home language and the intersection of Python with Rust is where I
think it really gets interesting. The cliche is true, came for the language, stayed
for the community. I've had the pleasure of meeting Pythonistas around the world,
from New Zealand, to Asia and then to Europe.

Most of my spare energy goes into the Python community in Tokyo: I run
[Tokyo Python](https://www.meetup.com/tokyopython/), an English speaking
Python user group here, and recently I've been
volunteering for [PyCon JP](https://www.pycon.jp/).

Cycling is the other constant. I've been a road cyclist for years: rule
#5, early starts, strong opinions about tyre inflation. I'm a bit more chilled
these days. Rural Japan doesn't have quite the same cycling scene as London or
Hong Kong, but I knew from an early age that cycling is something that I will
do for the rest of my life.

Of course, being a kiwi, I'm a keen rugby fan. My local team is the
[Saitama Wild Knights](https://www.wildknights.jp/), _consistently_ the
best team in Japan.

The rest of this site is my notebook: things I'm building, things that broke,
and the occasional half-formed idea. Everything here is my own opinion.
